Full Case Study / TVC KV Campaign

Shokubutsu
Campaign
System.

Body-care campaign visuals built to feel fresh, soft, and commercial across TVC, key visual, and rollout touchpoints. This page is the first Work Detail template for the portfolio system.

BrandShokubutsu
RoleArt Direction
ScopeTVC / KV / Rollout
FormatCampaign System
01

Context

The job is not only to make one key visual look good. It has to become a visual system that can travel.

For a body-care campaign, the visual language needs to balance product freshness, tactile softness, and commercial readability. The campaign needs to work as a strong hero image first, then survive across smaller assets, social formats, and rollout touchpoints.

This template keeps the writing intentionally sharp and expandable: when final case-study notes are ready, each section can be deepened without changing the page structure.

02

Idea

A fresh body-care world with soft product energy, clear campaign hierarchy, and enough structure to extend beyond the main visual.

The direction leans into a clean commercial mood rather than decorative beauty visuals only. The goal is to keep the product, benefit, and campaign feeling visible at a glance.

Visual Tone

Soft, fresh, body-care commercial mood with a clear hero-product presence.

Campaign Role

Built around a main KV that can support TVC, social, and retail-adjacent outputs.

Portfolio Value

Shows art direction, campaign thinking, and rollout discipline in one case.

03

Outputs

Key Visual

Main visual system and campaign mood anchor.

Rollout Logic

Compact asset area reserved for future rollout examples.

04

Takeaway

This case should sell the ability to think in campaign systems, not just single artworks.

The current page is a working template. Once more Shokubutsu assets are added, this can expand into image sequences, TVC frames, motion snippets, and rollout comparisons without changing the underlying UX structure.
